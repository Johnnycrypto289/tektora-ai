How to use this page

Do not think in software categories. Think in business outcomes. Each package below shows what level of system you are buying, what it feels like in plain English, and what a real example could look like.

If you want a cleaner website and better automations, that is Starter. If you need software shaped around your business, that is Business Update. If you want a fuller operating system with multiple AI roles, that is Full Build. If your scope is broader than a normal package, that is Custom.
