Tier-to-product map

Full Build is not a separate product line. It is the build tier for when several product systems need to run as one layer. Luxe Home is the clearest example in the repo because the build combines front-end response, follow-up logic, schedule movement, and homeowner communication.

Front-edge response

AI Receptionist gives the business faster first contact, tighter routing, and cleaner intake before work is booked.

Sales and follow-up

Lead Follow-Up System keeps opportunities moving so qualified jobs do not stall between contact, estimate, and next step.

Operations handoff

Scheduling & Dispatch System keeps install coordination, internal visibility, and homeowner updates tied to the same operating layer.

Positioning proof

This example keeps the product-to-tier logic explicit. AI Receptionist can support Starter when the front edge is still the issue. Lead Follow-Up System or Scheduling & Dispatch System can each justify Business Update when one workflow still explains the pain.

Luxe Home moves past both of those because those systems now have to work together. That is why this page stays pinned to Full Build and acts as the proof case for the boundary on the Products page.
