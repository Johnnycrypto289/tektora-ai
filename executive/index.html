• there are multiple pain points but no clear first move

• the stack is messy

• the operator is serious but unclear

• you need diagnosis before prescribing scope

4.2 Recommend Starter when

• one contained bottleneck is obvious

• complexity is low

• integration needs are light

• the buyer needs a first owned system, not a broader rebuild

4.3 Recommend Growth when

• one major workflow explains most of the pain

• the buyer needs something meaningfully custom

• one focused AI role makes sense

• the workflow needs rebuilding, not patching

• multiple workflows or handoffs are failing together

• the owner wants one operating layer rather than isolated fixes
