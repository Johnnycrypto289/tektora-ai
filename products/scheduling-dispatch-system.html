Scheduling & Dispatch System

Coordinates routing, calendar logic, and daily field movement for growing service teams.

What it is

Coordinates routing, calendar logic, and daily field movement for growing service teams.

Who it’s for

This system is best for Service businesses running multiple calls or jobs daily. It is strongest when speed, consistency, and cleaner handoffs matter more than patching together another subscription.

It usually sits in Business Update when dispatch, routing, and field coordination are the one core workflow that needs a real operating system. It moves into Full Build once scheduling has to stay tightly connected with intake, customer updates, and broader delivery handoffs across the business.

Best fitYou can point to scheduling and dispatch as the main bottleneck and need one stronger workflow system to control routing, calendar logic, and field execution.
Not for you ifYou only need a front-end refresh, or dispatch now has to coordinate tightly with lead intake, quoting, customer updates, and other workflows in one connected layer.

What problem it solves

Most contractors do not struggle because they lack effort. They struggle because the workflow breaks between inquiry, admin, scheduling, customer communication, and follow-up. Scheduling & Dispatch System tightens that part of the system.

What it can replace

  • Disconnected SaaS tools doing one part of the workflow
  • Manual handoffs between office staff and customers
  • Slow or inconsistent follow-up
  • Workarounds that depend on people remembering every step

What’s included

  • Workflow design around how your business actually operates
  • Configuration and implementation for your use case
  • Ownership-focused delivery
  • Support window based on tier fit

Typical outcomes

  • Faster response and cleaner execution
  • Less admin drag
  • Fewer dropped handoffs
  • A system that fits your process instead of forcing a workaround

Pricing fit

Usually Business Update. Move this into Full Build when dispatch is no longer a standalone workflow and the real problem is the handoff between intake, scheduling, field delivery, and customer communication.

Boundary check: stay in Business Update when scheduling and dispatch are still the one workflow doing most of the damage. If dispatch has to stay tied to intake, quoting, customer updates, and wider delivery handoffs in one operating layer, the honest read is Full Build like Luxe Home Services.
