What a Digital Workforce actually means

MEET YOUR DIGITAL WORKFORCE

AI roles built around real functions

These are not fantasy agents. These are practical function-based AI roles that support the way contractors actually operate.

Inbound Qualification Agent

Responds to new leads instantly, asks the right questions, filters bad fits, and books qualified prospects into the next step.

Target outcome: More qualified booked appointments each week

Dispatch & Logistics Agent

Coordinates scheduling, routes jobs, and keeps field communication cleaner when plans shift.

Target outcome: Fewer scheduling gaps and fewer update calls

Invoice & Collections Agent

Generates invoices, tracks payment status, and reduces repetitive back-office work after jobs are complete.

Target outcome: Faster billing and less admin drag

Follow-Up & Review Agent

Checks in after the job, requests reviews, catches unhappy customers early, and escalates issues before they grow.

Target outcome: More reviews and fewer unresolved issues

Internal Ops Agent

Moves information between systems, updates records, and handles repetitive workflow tasks your team should not be stuck doing manually.

Target outcome: Hours back every week for higher-value work

Customer Update Agent

Keeps customers informed about timing, delays, next steps, and what is happening without office staff manually chasing every update.

Target outcome: Better customer communication with less overhead
