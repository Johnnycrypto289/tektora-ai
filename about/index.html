ABOUT TEKTORA

Built by People Who Got Tired of Renting the Systems They Depend On

Tektora exists to help contractors and home service businesses own the systems that run their operations instead of paying forever for disconnected software they do not control.

WHY WE STARTED

We saw too many businesses building their operations on software they never actually own.

They pay more every year, shape their workflow around someone else’s product, and end up trapped inside systems they cannot truly control.

We started in the contractor world because that pain is obvious there — missed calls, bloated CRMs, clunky dispatch tools, and years of business history locked inside subscriptions.

Tektora exists to change that. We currently focus primarily on contractors and home service businesses, building custom apps, automations, and AI-powered systems that are clean, practical, and built to be owned.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Clear principles. Practical systems.

  • The system that runs your business should belong to you.
  • Software should fit the way you actually work — not force you into someone else’s process.
  • AI should remove friction, speed up operations, and create independence.
  • What we build should feel premium, work in the real world, and hold up over time.

HOW WE WORK

Built for contractors who need practical systems, not AI theater.

We do not start with hype. We start with bottlenecks. Where are leads leaking? Where is admin dragging the team down? Where are handoffs failing? What part of the stack is costing the most every month?

Then we build around the business: one painful bottleneck first, then a connected system, then — when the company is ready — a Digital Workforce that supports sales, follow-up, operations, finance, and customer communication.
