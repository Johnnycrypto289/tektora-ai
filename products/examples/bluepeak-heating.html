Tier boundary

BluePeak is the Starter reference because the business still mainly needs a stronger front edge, not a deeper custom workflow or a connected multi-workflow operating layer.

Why it stays in StarterThe main unlock is a better website, faster response, and light automation that makes the business feel sharper without rebuilding the operation.
Why it is not Business UpdateNo single workflow has become the obvious operational bottleneck yet. The website and response layer are still doing most of the damage.
Why it is not Full BuildThe pain does not live in the handoff between sales, scheduling, and delivery. One connected operating layer would be too much scope for the real problem.
Move up whenOne workflow like intake, follow-up, quoting, or dispatch becomes the main problem instead of the front-end experience.
