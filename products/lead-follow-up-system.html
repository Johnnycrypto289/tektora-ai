Lead Follow-Up System

Automates lead follow-up so warm opportunities do not go cold between inquiry and booked job.

What it is

Automates lead follow-up so warm opportunities do not go cold between inquiry and booked job.

Who it’s for

This system is best for Contractors losing jobs to slow response. It is strongest when speed, consistency, and cleaner handoffs matter more than patching together another subscription.

If lead follow-up is the main broken process, this is usually a Business Update decision because one workflow needs its own real system. It only stays in Starter when the need is still light automation around a broader front-end refresh. It moves into Full Build once follow-up has to stay tightly connected with scheduling, install coordination, and homeowner updates across the business.

Best fitYou can point to one follow-up bottleneck that is costing jobs and needs its own custom system with tighter logic.
Not for you ifYou only need a basic website refresh, or fixing follow-up alone would still leave scheduling, install coordination, and customer updates disconnected.

What problem it solves

Most contractors do not struggle because they lack effort. They struggle because the workflow breaks between inquiry, admin, scheduling, customer communication, and follow-up. Lead Follow-Up System tightens that part of the system.

What it can replace

  • Disconnected SaaS tools doing one part of the workflow
  • Manual handoffs between office staff and customers
  • Slow or inconsistent follow-up
  • Workarounds that depend on people remembering every step

What’s included

  • Workflow design around how your business actually operates
  • Configuration and implementation for your use case
  • Ownership-focused delivery
  • Support window based on tier fit

Typical outcomes

  • Faster response and cleaner execution
  • Less admin drag
  • Fewer dropped handoffs
  • A system that fits your process instead of forcing a workaround

Pricing fit

Usually Business Update. Keep it in Starter only when follow-up is a light automation attached to a broader front-end upgrade instead of the main workflow problem. Move it into Full Build when the real issue is no longer one follow-up workflow, but the handoff between follow-up, scheduling, install coordination, and customer communication.

Boundary check: stay in Business Update when one follow-up workflow still explains most of the pain. If fixing follow-up alone would still leave scheduling, install coordination, and homeowner updates disconnected, the honest next step is Full Build like Luxe Home Services, not a softer middle tier.
