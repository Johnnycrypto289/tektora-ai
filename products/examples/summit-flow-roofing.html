Tier boundary

Summit Flow is the Business Update reference because one workflow still explains the pain honestly. The fix is a stronger system around that bottleneck, not a broader operating layer across the whole business.

Why it is not StarterThe front end alone will not solve this. One broken process now needs its own custom operating system behind it.
Why it stays in Business UpdateThe project can still be scoped around one workflow with one active AI role instead of several connected systems.
Why it is not Full BuildThe handoff between workflows is not the main problem yet. Rebuilding one workflow would still remove most of the pain.
Move up whenSales, scheduling, delivery, and customer updates start breaking in the space between workflows instead of inside one bottleneck.
